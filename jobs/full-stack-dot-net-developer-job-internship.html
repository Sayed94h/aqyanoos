  • Collaborate with team members to design and implement software solutions.
  • Participate in code reviews, identify areas of improvement, and implement best practices.

  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.

What we offer:

  • Extra boost / motivation to help you become a problem solver, smarter and a better software developer.
