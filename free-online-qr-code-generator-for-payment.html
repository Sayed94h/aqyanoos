With this Free online QR-Code Generator you can create QR-Code for payment and it has made the payment and money request easy for your clients or customers, friends, family,...

Send money request or send payment request using QR-Code. This way your clients, customers, friends, family can pay the money with one scan. It is a quick way to make the payment or pay the money for the products or services. This QR-Code Generator is fully customizable. Enter Your Payment Details

BIC Code or SWIFT Code is mostly used for international bank payment / transfer. Every bank has a unique BIC code that you can search on Google "BankName SWIFT". In Europe they call it BIC but in America they call it SWIFT.
It's possible that the Date will not work But it can be changed on the bank App after scanning the QR-Code.
Structured Message is used in payments to identify a payment programmatically. It makes the payment unique. It's something like this: +++101/20235/503+++ or an usual text.

How does it work?

It works only if you scan the QR-Code using your bank App, that is why this QR-Code generator is super secure and safe. Normally, all bank Apps have the functionality to scan QR-Code images for payment (reading the payment details from the QR-Code and filling the payment form automatically). We don't collect any of your information and we don't redirect you to any website when you scan the QR-Code.

After scanning the QR-Code with your bank App if it does not work, then please contact us so that we fix and improve it for you. We have tested the QR-Code on most bank Apps.
